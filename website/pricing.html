Common questions

Is PRISM really ₹999 for the whole agency?

Yes. One subscription covers your entire agency — no per-seat pricing, no matter how many people use it. We think per-seat pricing is a bad deal for agencies.

Does my data leave my machine?

Your database data and Excel files never leave your machine. The only thing that goes to our AI server is the question you type (to convert it to SQL or pandas). Query results stay local. Database passwords are encrypted with a private key.

What databases does PRISM support?

PostgreSQL, MySQL, and MariaDB. More databases (SQLite, MS SQL Server) are on the roadmap. Excel and CSV files work without any database connection at all.

What file types can I upload?

Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV files. You can upload multiple files in one session and query across all of them together — perfect for combining Meta, Google, and Instagram exports.

What happens when the free beta ends?

We'll give you at least 30 days notice before switching to paid. Early beta users get a discount. We're not going to pull the rug.

How do I pay? Is Razorpay supported?

Payments will be processed via Razorpay — UPI, cards, net banking all supported. Payment integration is coming in Month 2 of our roadmap.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your settings page and your subscription ends at the period end. No questions asked, no retention dark patterns.
