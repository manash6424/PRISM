Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel from your settings page at any time. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing month — then drop back to Free. No charges after cancellation.

Which payment methods are supported?

All major UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking, and wallets — powered by Razorpay, India's most trusted payment gateway.

Is my data safe?

PRISM never stores your raw data. Database credentials are AES-256 encrypted. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay — we never see your card details.

Do you offer refunds?

If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days of your first subscription, contact us at support@prism.app for a full refund. After that, refunds are on a case-by-case basis.

Can I switch plans?

Yes — upgrade from Pro to Team at any time. The difference is prorated for your current billing period.
