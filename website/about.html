Our Story

Built out of frustration.
Designed for agencies.

We were tired of watching talented marketers waste every Monday morning copy-pasting numbers into Excel. So we built PRISM.

Why PRISM exists

Indian digital marketing agencies are incredibly capable — they run multi-crore campaigns for brands across Meta, Google, and Instagram. But when Monday comes, the same ritual plays out at every agency: pull data from each platform, paste it into Excel, build the charts, write the analysis, and send the report.

That's 4 hours of work that should take 10 minutes. And the person doing it is usually the most experienced person in the room — the one who should be thinking about strategy, not copy-pasting numbers.

PRISM exists to give those 4 hours back. Ask your question in plain English. Get your answer in seconds. Send the report. Done.

Our mission

We're not trying to compete with ChatGPT on intelligence or Tableau on feature count. PRISM has one job: be the Monday morning reporting tool for Indian agencies. Purpose-built, 10x cheaper than enterprise BI, zero training needed.

By September 2026, we want 500 agencies spending 10 minutes on what used to take 4 hours.

The tech

PRISM runs as a desktop app (Windows first, macOS coming) built on Electron. The AI engine uses Groq's llama-3.3-70b model to convert plain English into SQL or pandas code. Your data never leaves your machine — we connect directly to your database or read your uploaded files locally.

DesktopElectron (Node.js)
FrontendHTML / CSS / Vanilla JS + Chart.js
BackendFastAPI + Python
AI EngineGroq — llama-3.3-70b-versatile
Auth & DataSupabase (PostgreSQL + JWT)
Processingpandas + openpyxl

Auth and user data are stored securely on Supabase. Database passwords are encrypted. The app works offline for file uploads and only needs internet for AI queries.
