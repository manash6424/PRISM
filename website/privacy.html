Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Applies to PRISM desktop app and website

🔒 The short version: your database data and uploaded files never leave your machine. The only thing that reaches our servers is the plain-English question you type — nothing else.

1. Who we are

PRISM is a desktop application built by Manash Mandal and Shreya Kolam for Indian digital marketing agencies. We are based in India. You can reach us at the contact details at the bottom of this page.

2. What data we collect

We collect the minimum required to run the product.

  • Account information — your email address and a hashed password, stored securely on Supabase.
  • Natural language queries — the plain-English questions you type are sent to Groq's API to be converted into SQL or pandas code. Groq's privacy policy applies to this data.
  • Database connection metadata — host, port, database name, and username are stored (encrypted) in Supabase so your connections survive app restarts. Passwords are encrypted with a private key before storage.
  • Usage data — query history and saved queries are stored locally on your machine in the app's local storage.

3. What data we do NOT collect

This is the important part.

  • The contents of your database — query results stay on your machine.
  • The contents of uploaded Excel or CSV files — file processing happens locally via pandas.
  • Any client data your agency manages.
  • Any payment information — payments are handled entirely by Razorpay.

4. How we use your data

  • To authenticate you and manage your session (Supabase Auth).
  • To convert your natural language questions into SQL or pandas code (Groq API).
  • To restore your saved database connections across sessions.
  • To send alerts you configure (email or Slack) when thresholds are crossed.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not share it with third parties beyond the services named above (Supabase, Groq, Razorpay).

5. Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication and encrypted connection storage. Supabase Privacy Policy →
  • Groq — AI inference for NL → SQL conversion. Groq Privacy Policy →
  • Razorpay — payment processing (coming Month 2). Razorpay Privacy Policy →

6. Data retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. You can request deletion at any time by emailing us and we will remove your account and associated data within 7 business days.

Query history and dashboard pins are stored locally on your machine. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

7. Security

Database passwords are encrypted before being stored. All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) ensures each user can only access their own data.

No security system is perfect. If you discover a vulnerability, please email us directly rather than disclosing it publicly.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 7 business days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as PRISM grows. If we make significant changes, we will notify you via the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
